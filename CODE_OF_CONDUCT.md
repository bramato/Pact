# Code of conduct

PACT welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, discuss ideas with evidence, accept corrections, and give people room to learn.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable. Keep technical disagreement focused on the proposal and its effects.

This policy applies to project issues, reviews, discussions, and other spaces managed by the maintainers. To report a problem, contact a repository maintainer through a private channel listed on their GitHub profile. Do not post private details in public issues. Maintainers should acknowledge reports, protect confidentiality where possible, and assess the evidence fairly.

Responses may include a request to change behavior, removal of content, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban according to severity and repeated conduct. A maintainer involved in the report should recuse themselves. This is a project-specific policy; it does not imply membership in another project's governance.
